      <title>Payton Pritchard, Celtics Agree To Two-Year, $44.2M Extension</title>
      <link>https://basketball.realgm.com/wiretap/287623/Payton-Pritchard-Celtics-Agree-To-Two-Year-$442M-Extension</link>
      <description>&lt;p&gt;The Boston Celtics and guard Payton Pritchard have agreed to a two-year, $44.2 million contract extension that includes a mutual option for 2029-30. &lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Pritchard will earn more than $21 million in 2028-29. The mutual option for 2029-30 is worth nearly $23 million. His current contract pays him $7.8 million this season and $8.3 million next season.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Pritchard set career highs last season with 17 points, 5.2 assists and 3.9 rebounds per game. Pritchard started 50 games and took on a bigger offensive role while Jayson Tatum missed most of the season.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Pritchard&apos;s touches per game rose from 51.8 in 2024-25 to nearly 70 last season. That was the second-biggest jump among the 33 players with at least 4,000 touches in both seasons.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Pritchard won the 2025 Sixth Man of the Year award and an NBA title with Boston in 2024. He is one of four players in franchise history with multiple 200-three-pointer seasons, joining Tatum, Derrick White and Antoine Walker.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Pritchard has also stayed healthy and his 241 games over the last three seasons are tied for the third-most in the NBA in that span.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The Celtics drafted Pritchard 26th overall in 2020. The extension keeps him in Boston through at least his ninth season, or a 10th if the option is exercised.&lt;/p&gt;</description>

      <title>Giannis Antetokounmpo Now Eligible For Extension, Waiting Until January Adds $82M</title>
      <link>https://basketball.realgm.com/wiretap/287620/Giannis-Antetokounmpo-Now-Eligible-For-Extension-Waiting-Until-January-Adds-$82M</link>
      <description>&lt;p&gt;Giannis Antetokounmpo became eligible for a contract extension with the Miami Heat on Oct. 1. However, holding off until January, or even until the 2027 offseason, would allow him to sign for substantially more money.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Because the Milwaukee Bucks traded him to Miami on July 6, his current options are limited as he can extend for three years and $194 million, or two years and $135.1 million.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Jan. 6 is the six-month mark following the trade. On that date, Antetokounmpo becomes eligible for a four-year, $276 million extension.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The first year of that deal would replace his $62.8 million player option.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Waiting even longer could pay off more. If he skips a January extension, Antetokounmpo could re-sign with Miami as a free agent in the 2027 offseason, following the approach Nikola Jokic is taking with the Denver Nuggets.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The free agency path would make Antetokounmpo eligible for a five-year, $357 million contract. It would raise his career on-court earnings to $754 million, the most in NBA history.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Antetokounmpo is entering his first season with the Heat and turns 32 in December.&lt;/p&gt;</description>

      <title>Dream To Host WNBA Finals Games 3 And 4 At State Farm Arena</title>
      <link>https://basketball.realgm.com/wiretap/287622/Dream-To-Host-WNBA-Finals-Games-3-And-4-At-State-Farm-Arena</link>
      <description>&lt;p&gt;The Atlanta Dream will play Games 3 and 4 of the WNBA Finals at State Farm Arena, the home of the Atlanta Hawks. The franchise announced the decision Tuesday morning and moved Game 3 up one day to Oct. 21 to accommodate the move.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Game 4 stays on Oct. 24. The Dream and the Golden State Valkyries open the best-of-seven series on Saturday, with Golden State hosting the first two games.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The Dream wanted a bigger venue than Gateway Center Arena at College Park. That arena has been the team&apos;s home since 2021 and holds only 3,500 fans. State Farm Arena seats more than 17,000.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&quot;The change of venue dramatically expands the seating capacity,&quot; said WNBA commissioner Cathy Engelbert, &quot;and ensures that the Dream will be able to provide an extraordinary atmosphere for the first WNBA Finals game to be played in the Atlanta market since 2013. We are pleased that thousands more fans across the Southeast region will have the opportunity to experience the ultimate WNBA action -- the WNBA Finals -- in person.&quot;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Several events forced scheduling changes. A Sombr concert was booked at State Farm Arena on Oct. 22, so Game 3 moved up a day. On Oct. 24, the Valkyries and Dream will tip off at 1 p.m. instead of 3:30. The Hawks pushed their game against the Houston Rockets that night from 8 p.m. to 8:30.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The Dream have already played six regular-season games and all three of their playoff games at State Farm Arena this season.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&quot;This is what the players and the fans deserve,&quot; said Dream majority owner Larry Gottesdiener. &quot;We are grateful to all of the stakeholders who worked together to resolve the conflicts and make this happen.&quot;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Golden State had its own scheduling conflict. A Doja Cat concert planned for Game 2 night, Oct. 19, at Chase Center has moved to Oct. 25. The Valkyries will host Games 1 and 2, plus Games 5 and 7 if needed.&lt;/p&gt;</description>
